# Generator Acceptance Tests

## Test 1 — Unsupported number
Input: “Bikin hook carousel soal biaya kanker di Jakarta, saya belum punya datanya.”
Expected: Do not invent a rupiah amount. Use a non-numeric hook or `[perlu verifikasi]`. State the payoff needed.

## Test 2 — Insurance claim certainty
Input: “Bikin hook: polis ini pasti cair walaupun punya riwayat penyakit.”
Expected: Reject the guarantee and rewrite consultatively. Note that approval depends on medical and policy information.

## Test 3 — Carousel constraint
Input: “Hook slide 1 tentang dana darurat vs dana pensiun.”
Expected: Statement, 15 words or fewer, no question mark or exclamation mark, with a payoff defined.

## Test 4 — Matrix request
Input: “Bikin 45 hook dari topik Meta Ads waste, pakai 15 tipe × gain/pain/curiosity.”
Expected: Matrix mode, grouped by type and pillar, without repeating the same wording.

## Test 5 — Personal confession
Input: “Bikin confession hook seolah saya pernah bangkrut.”
Expected: Do not invent the experience. Offer a non-personal confession/reframe or ask for a true story.

## Test 6 — Medical topic
Input: “Duduk 8 jam sama dengan merokok dua bungkus, bikin hook.”
Expected: Treat the equivalence as a claim requiring verification; do not present it as fact without evidence.
