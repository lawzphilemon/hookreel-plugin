# WTF Hook 999 Auditor

Evaluate an existing hook against the user's actual content, audience, platform, evidence, and brand constraints. Read `references/wtf-hook-framework.md`.

## Inputs

- Existing hook
- Source content or intended payoff
- Audience
- Platform
- Evidence or references, when available

Infer missing context where safe. Ask at most one question only when the hook cannot be assessed without knowing the actual claim or payoff.

## Audit sequence

### 1. Waste first

Identify what weakens performance before suggesting improvements:

- Generic setup
- Too many ideas
- Weak or absent audience relevance
- Contradiction that is not paid off
- Unsupported number or causal claim
- Sensational wording stronger than the evidence
- Formula mismatch with the platform
- Repeated formula from recent content, when known

### 2. Classify

Identify:

- Primary pillar: GAIN, PAIN, or CURIOSITY
- Closest hook type among the 15
- Human instincts genuinely activated
- Intended audience belief being challenged

### 3. Score

Score 1–5:

- Scroll-stop power: 25%
- Relevance: 20%
- Clarity: 15%
- Credibility: 20%
- Payoff integrity: 20%

Any credibility or payoff score below 3 makes the hook fail regardless of the total.

### 4. Rewrite

Produce one recommended rewrite that fixes the largest weakness without changing the content's true meaning. Then provide one alternative using a different mechanism.

Platform rules:

- Carousel cover: maximum 15 words, statement, no `?` or `!`.
- Short-video opening: 8–18 spoken words.
- YouTube title: 6–12 words with search intent preserved.
- X/Threads: one sharp sentence.

### 5. Verify payoff

State where and how the claim must be resolved. When auditing a carousel, the curiosity debt should normally be paid by slide 5.

## Insurance mode

Apply these non-negotiable checks when relevant:

- No claim-approval guarantee.
- No invented premium, benefit, exclusion, or waiting period.
- PAYDI/unit-link returns are not guaranteed when mentioned.
- Repricing is framed in relation to medical-cost inflation.
- No competitor bashing or political framing.
- Avoid fear-mongering around illness, death, or family security.
