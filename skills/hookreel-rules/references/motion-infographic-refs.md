## Patterns seen across the set

- **One idea, one visual, under 15 s** wins reach: the top data reels (6.1M, 1.07M, 328k) are 5–16 s with one metaphor each. Matches hookreel's one-visual-per-scene rule.
- **Metaphor over digits:** the biggest reel shows a ratio with particles and a funnel, no number on screen. Use `bars`, `split` or `dots` when the exact figure matters less than the gap.
- **One accent on a quiet base:** white, cream or navy backgrounds; one colour (red line, gold particles, neon green) marks what matters. Same as the house rule.
- **Text lands on the spoken word:** the hand-drawn Indonesian explainer writes each label as it is said. That is what hookreel cues already do; keep cues tight.
- **Gaps in the catalog** these suggest: a `map` (grey base, one route or region lit), a `radar`/profile chart, a `line` chart that draws in. Not built yet; use `flow` or `bars` until they are.
